Fire Message is the fastest way to send, test, and debug push notifications for your Firebase-powered apps.

Built for developers and QA teams, Fire Message gives you full control over the messaging HTTP v1 API without touching a terminal or writing a script.

MANAGE EVERY PROJECT
Add as many projects as you need and switch between Dev, Staging, and Production instantly. Keep every environment organized in one place.

SEND MESSAGES IN SECONDS
Compose and send push notifications with full control over notification text, custom data payloads, and delivery options — all from a clean, simple interface.

PRECISE TARGETING
Send to a single device token, a topic, or a condition. Test exactly the audience your production app will reach before you ship.

FULL SEND HISTORY
Every message you send is logged with its response. Review past sends, duplicate a payload to reuse it, and resend with one tap.

SECURITY FIRST
Your service account credentials never leave your device. Keys are encrypted and stored locally — nothing is uploaded to any external server.

Whether you're debugging a notification issue, demoing push behavior to a client, or running QA before a release, Fire Message keeps your entire workflow in your pocket.
